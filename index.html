Welcome to Shreekamal

Rooted in farming, built on nutrition science

Shreekamal grew out of a long family trade in cattle feed and oil, carried forward with modern manufacturing and a simple belief — better feed means healthier animals and more income for the farmer.

Before balanced feed became common, farmers relied only on hay and grass, which rarely met an animal's full nutritional needs. Today Shreekamal produces balanced cattle feed, edible oils and protein-rich oil cakes, supplying dairy farmers, distributors and traders across several Indian states.

  • Hygienic, safe feed made from the finest raw materials
  • Balanced formulas for milk, growth and reproduction
  • Reliable supply and on-time delivery, every season

Infrastructure

DORB Solvent Plant & Modern Milling

We installed our own de-oiled rice bran (DORB) solvent plant for one reason — to control quality at the source. It lets us secure good raw material, protect Shreekamal's reputation in the market and avoid paying over the odds for inputs.

Combined with modern feed milling and oil extraction lines, our infrastructure supports high daily capacity without compromising on consistency.
