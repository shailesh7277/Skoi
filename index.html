Founded in 2011, Growing Strong for Over a Decade

Leading in Cattle Feed & Edible Oils

Shreekamal Oil Industries traces its roots to a family trade in cattle feed and oil that began decades ago, and was formally established in 2011 at Hinganghat, Maharashtra — on a strong foundation of trust and quality.

From a modest manual operation, the business has grown into a modern, largely automated plant, expanding into balanced cattle feed, edible oils and protein-rich oil cakes. With our own DORB solvent unit and modern milling lines, we control quality from raw material to finished bag — serving farmers and dealers across 7 states.

Why Choose Shreekamal?

We integrate modern technology, strict quality standards and a dedicated R&D team to produce hygienic, safe cattle feed of the highest quality. Crafted from premium raw materials, our products are designed to meet the nutritional needs of cattle while ensuring cost efficiency — making Shreekamal a trusted choice for superior cattle nutrition.

Benefits of Feeding Shreekamal

Shreekamal feed bridges the nutritional gaps left by traditional feeding, ensuring your cattle receive complete nourishment. It boosts milk production, enhances fat content and strengthens immunity while supporting overall growth and fertility — promoting healthier, more productive animals season after season.
