DORB Solvent Plant

We installed our own de-oiled rice bran (DORB) solvent plant for one clear reason — to control quality at the source. It helps us secure good raw material, protect Shreekamal’s standing in the market and avoid paying over the odds for inputs.

This control over raw material is what keeps our feed consistent and our pricing fair, batch after batch.
