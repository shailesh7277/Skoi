Meet Our Founder

Sh. Suresh Singhal Ji

Founder & Director of Shreekamal Oil Industries — a vision born from the soil, shaped by trust, and driven by results.

Our founder, Sh. Suresh Singhal Ji, grew up close to farming and saw first-hand how much a farmer's livelihood depends on healthy, productive animals. At a time when cattle were fed mainly hay and grass — rarely meeting their real nutritional needs — he set out to change things, building a deep understanding of cattle feed and the edible-oil trade.

Starting from a modest family operation, he grew the business step by step by educating farmers about the benefits of balanced, complete nutrition. As awareness spread, so did demand from farmers and dairy owners — driving steady expansion into cattle feed, edible oils and oil cakes.

With an unwavering commitment to the philosophy of "Quality First," he shaped Shreekamal Oil Industries into a trusted name across several states — a legacy of purity, trust and growth that the next generation continues to carry forward.

Our Vision

To build a strong supplier network and continually raise our customer-satisfaction standards, expanding from domestic markets toward the international stage.

Our Mission

To become a leading name in the domestic and international agro industry, turning our productivity into shared prosperity for farmers, dealers and every stakeholder.
